## Stage 0 exit gate

Do not declare Stage 0 complete until all three conditions are supported by first-party evidence:

- [ ] 3–5 qualified external B2B SaaS design partners actively complete the agreed workflow.
- [ ] At least 1 verified paid pilot is collected or supported by a signed commercial commitment.
- [ ] At least 2 organizations complete a second comparable reviewed cycle.

Everything else is subordinate to this gate.

## Initial ICP experiment

**Company:** English-language B2B SaaS, roughly 50–500 employees, at least three direct competitors, and an internal team that can act on evidence.

**Champion hypothesis:** Head/Director of SEO, Organic Growth, Content, GEO, AEO, or Growth Marketing.

**Economic buyer hypothesis:** VP Marketing, CMO, or VP Growth with budget authority.

Revisit this ICP only after at least 20 qualified conversations produce a clear contradiction.

## Founder-led commercial motion

1. **Target:** account fits the ICP and has a publicly verifiable trigger.
2. **Contacted:** one founder-led message sent with a real reason for relevance; no fabricated personalization.
3. **Conversation:** prospect engages in a real problem discussion.
4. **Qualified:** pain, urgency, authority, ability to act, and scope fit are confirmed.
5. **Demo:** 20 minutes around one buyer question, one Recommendation Record, one evidence review, and one Change Specification. Do not tour the whole product.
6. **Proposal:** one narrow 30-day pilot: five questions, 3–5 competitors, baseline, one reviewed action, comparable remeasurement.
7. **Pilot:** commercial commitment is signed or payment is collected. List price alone is not proof.
8. **Activated:** customer reaches the defined reviewed decision insight and owned change.
9. **Second cycle:** comparable remeasurement is completed.
10. **Continuation:** explicit continue, renew, expand, refer, or lost decision is recorded.

## Pricing experiment

The $3,000 and $6,000 pilot amounts are **hypotheses**, not validated prices and not public pricing.

For every proposal record separately:

- list/hypothesis price;
- offered price;
- objection;
- negotiation;
- accepted contracted value;
- collected cash;
- lost reason.

Never convert pipeline, verbal interest, an invoice, or a list price into collected revenue.

## Roadmap freeze

Do not build these during Stage 0 unless new customer/production evidence changes the gate:

- broad marketplace;
- public benchmark;
- financing layer;
- generic marketing platform;
- second country or multilingual product;
- large sales team;
- additional top-level product objects;
- another analytics truth store;
- broad autonomous-agent suite;
- unsupported category-leadership, ROI, logo, or benchmark claims.

## Kill / pivot triggers

Stop feature expansion and investigate if any of these become true:

- after 30 qualified interviews the problem is consistently low urgency, unowned, or unfunded;
- after 100 qualified target accounts and disciplined outreach there is no meaningful conversation rate and messaging tests do not improve it;
- customers can see measurements but do not approve or execute any company change;
- activated customers do not return for a second comparable cycle because value is absent rather than because workflow friction blocks them;
- prospects consistently prefer a simpler deliverable that does not require the current platform breadth;
- provider instability or comparability prevents a trustworthy repeated workflow at acceptable cost.

A trigger is a decision point, not an automatic shutdown. Narrow the ICP, change the job, simplify the product, change the business model, or exit the thesis based on evidence.
