## What binds first

1. **Founder review time**, not infrastructure: ~32 h/month of evidence review at 20 orgs on a weekly cadence. Biweekly cadence halves it. This is the real 20-org gate.
2. **Workers Free 10 ms CPU** (A17): production p50 CPU on successful invocations was 11.2 ms and p99 138.9 ms over 7 days (`a17-runtime-503.md`) — the limit is already exceeded on many requests and tolerated by isolate burst allowance; 1102/503 incidence will rise with authenticated traffic. Workers Paid removes the 10 ms limit (owner spend decision).
3. **No backup** (see `restore-and-rollback-plan.md`) — an availability/recovery risk that does not scale with orgs but makes every paid org exposed.
4. Inngest, Resend and DB storage stay well inside free limits through 20 orgs **if** retries stay near the measured 1.35x. A provider outage that drives every question to 3 attempts adds ≈ +2.6k executions at 20 orgs — still inside quota. Inngest Free **pauses** executions at quota rather than billing, so an overrun is an outage, not a charge: alert at 70%.
