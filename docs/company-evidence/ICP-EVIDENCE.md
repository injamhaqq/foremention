# Foremention ICP Evidence

Status: **🟡 DEFINED — NOT PROVEN**

Working hypothesis version: `ICP-2026-09-v1`

This file is the canonical distinction between the current ICP hypothesis and first-party evidence. A document saying something is true is not customer evidence.

## Truth labels

Every market statement must be labeled as one of:

- **VERIFIED FACT** — directly verified non-customer fact.
- **PRODUCTION FACT** — directly observed in the current production system.
- **FIRST-PARTY CUSTOMER EVIDENCE** — behavior, language, commitment, commercial fact, or product behavior from a real qualified buyer/account, linked to a source record.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — an assumption to test.
- **TARGET** — an operating threshold or objective, never a claim.
- **EXPERIMENT** — a planned/running test, never proof by itself.
- **UNKNOWN** — information we do not yet have.
- **BLOCKED** — information that cannot currently be verified because a required external/system dependency is unavailable.

## Problem hypothesis

- painful;
- strategic;
- budget-worthy;
- recurring;
- owned by a recognizable role;
- triggered by a recognizable event;

## Strongly ICP-relevant interview criteria

An interview can be marked `strong_icp` only when the account/contact substantially matches the current hypothesis and can speak from recent behavior. Prefer:

1. B2B software or a closely analogous considered-purchase market.
2. Meaningful competitive shortlist/category pressure.
3. Buyer questions that influence discovery, evaluation, or comparison.
4. A marketing/growth/organic/product-marketing function with ability to act.
5. The participant has personally seen, owned, investigated, budgeted, or escalated the problem.
6. Evidence is about a real recent incident, not general AI opinions.

Adjacent interviews are useful for contradiction/discovery but must not be silently counted as strongly ICP-relevant.

## First-party milestone targets

These are **TARGETS**, not proof thresholds that guarantee PMF:

- 15–20 strongly ICP-relevant buyer interviews;
- independent repetition of pain patterns;
- one buyer/champion combination beginning to emerge;
- repeated real trigger events;
- enough unaided category responses to judge comprehension honestly;
- qualified people volunteering urgency rather than being led toward it;
- real commitments of time/data/workflow;
- design partners reaching first reviewed Recommendation Record;
- organizations completing an exact-comparable second cycle;
- explicit WTP evidence;
- verified collected payment if/when a transaction occurs;
- engagement after payment;
- renewal, expansion, churn and referral recorded only when they actually happen.

Insufficient sample stays insufficient sample.

## Market-truth checkpoint

At each material evidence checkpoint choose one, with linked evidence:

- **KEEP** — evidence supports the hypothesis.
- **REFINE** — the underlying problem appears real but segmentation, buyer, message, or category needs narrowing.
- **PIVOT** — repeated evidence contradicts a major assumption and a materially different hypothesis is better supported.
- **KILL** — strong evidence indicates insufficient urgency/value for the target market.

Contradictory evidence is preserved, not averaged away.
