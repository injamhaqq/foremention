# Foremention — Competitive Intelligence and 90-Day Founder Execution
**Research cutoff: 30 September 2026.** Research-only, not a customer-data record, production capability claim, audited market share estimate or release authorization.
**This document is the working founder strategy, not a declaration that the positioning, customer segment or price has already been validated.**

## Executive choice and its falsifier
**Recommended hypothesis:** Own the narrow decision-evidence job for growth-stage, English-language B2B SaaS marketing/growth teams whose prospects ask consequential vendor comparison questions. Deliver a human-controlled workflow: freeze five consequential buyer questions → capture legally licensed responses/provenance → review evidence and limitations → record the team's accountable decision and approved company-owned Change Specification → execute → run a version-comparable second cycle → export an honest audit trail. Sell the repeatable *decision review and governance process*, not a visibility score or a guaranteed AI ranking improvement.

**Why this direction is conditional:** The largest competitors are moving rapidly beyond simple visibility charts into agentic action, source analysis, attribution and distribution. These observed moves invalidate any unverified claim that "we alone go from insight to action." The remaining hypothesis is that a specific purchaser needs a *defensible decision file*: what verified evidence existed, who approved which intervention, whether it was completed, and whether the next measurement used genuinely comparable conditions. It could still turn out to be a feature of a larger platform, a services deliverable, an agency workflow, or insufficiently valuable. Customer behavior decides.

**Disconfirm it:** When qualified prospects consistently solve the job using existing systems, cannot identify an economic buyer, won't commit to a second cycle, or prefer a simpler manual audit; do not expand product breadth to hide an unsuccessful wedge.

## Six strategic counter-moves, prioritized by founder effort
1. **Evidence admissibility before coverage:** An asserted official source must resolve to a genuine exact official HTTPS host and independently support the relevant answer. Domain provenance alone is necessary, not factual support. Mark unverifiable responses as unavailable, not as eight irrelevant citations. Commercial search/display/retention rights must be verified first (#345/#346).
2. **Human-accountable decision file:** In each pilot, preserve frozen question set, query/locale/model/protocol versions, the cited material that the team was *permitted* to retain, the reviewer, approved owner-controlled action and its delivery reference, and the uncertainty/limitations. Capture signed-off scope without pretending a dashboard predicts buyers.
3. **True like-for-like second cycle:** Fail closed on mismatched question/model/provider/context, incomplete answer sets, missing run aggregates or an ineligible latest cycle. Treat before/after association as non-causal. Report separate metrics for source support, false evidence, reliability, latency and total delivered cost.
4. **Service-assisted activation, product-led repetition:** Manually help each design partner configure five real buying questions and execute one defensible action. Automate only steps repeatedly validated by real partners, not speculative autonomous site changes or self-sent outreach.
5. **Distribution built from truth:** Use 15–20 customer-discovery interviews to test role/buyer, problem ownership and budget. Source public target-account triggers, but preserve the distinction between a *research candidate* and permissioned client/customer data. Request approval before contacting anyone or spending.
6. **Interoperability over feature breadth:** Customer-owned proof links and exports can connect to existing SEO, analytics, CMS and internal planning workflows once a concrete integration request recurs. No generic bot renderer, ad agent, broad model benchmark or marketplace expansion during Stage 0.

### Days 1–15: discover the paying job
- Prepare 100 public, trigger-specific *candidate* accounts from the existing research pool; independently re-check employer size, recent official announcement, competitors and likely responsible role. A candidate is **not** a verified contact or opt-in lead.
- Seek 15–20 strongly relevant interviews, including negative cases; log a real recent incident, current workaround, frequency, consequences, buyer, budget process, alternative and contradictions in the canonical restricted research ledger.
- Before anyone receives a message, require explicit founder approval of target list, content and sending scope. Prefer genuine introductions and specific 1:1 discovery rather than risky bulk unsolicited contact.
- Ask for **unaided** explanation of the current category name before providing any definition. Show the product workflow after recording the buyer's description.

### Days 16–30: prove one decision workflow
- Invite **3–5** qualified design partners who own a real comparison-question problem and can make one customer-owned change inside a month (target, not a claim).
- Founder-assisted, narrowly scoped pilot hypothesis: one brand, five fixed consequential buyer questions, 3–5 relevant competitors; one reviewed Recommendation Record, one human-approved Change Specification, one executed proof link.
- Define pilot failure/success before running: genuine source support, correct refusal when support is absent, first-value and work required, customer-acknowledged next action, owner of that action. Do not claim commercial licensed coverage until approved.

### Days 31–60: verify repeated value and unit economics
- Conduct exact-comparable second cycles at least one week apart (target at least two partners repeat within 30 days). Retain failures and incomparability, not just favorable deltas.
- Quantify fully loaded delivered cost: licensing, model tokens, collection retries, infrastructure, support and manual evidence-review time per **customer-reviewed decision**. Separately measure gross margin and product latency on real permissioned workflows.
- Test a paid pilot scope around an explicitly unvalidated ~$500 for four weeks only when a prospect has a real budget conversation. Track actual agreed price, invoice, receipt and continuation distinctly. No automatic billing claims.

### Days 61–90: decide on evidence
- Exit milestone **TARGET**: at least three genuine design partners, one independently confirmed payment, at least two genuinely comparable second cycles, and one repeated evidence-backed workflow that users would miss.
- Review activation-to-second-cycle, whether an approver used the decision file, paid retention intent and delivered cost. Unknown remains unknown; sample quality matters more than volume.
- If repeated customers value *source inspection* but not complex governance, simplify to a tightly scoped evidence review. If governance is valuable but five-question measurement is unreliable, fix protocol/licensed sources before expansion. If neither is budget worthy after adequate interviews, consider pivoting or ending the hypothesis.

## Positioning and sales discipline
**Testable problem-first opening:** “For the buying questions that matter, inspect what AI recommended and what the permitted evidence actually supports. Give your team a reviewed action and repeat the same measurement without changing the rules in the middle.”

**Don't say:** “We are the only insight-to-action platform”; “we guarantee rankings/revenue”; “we have representative market visibility from five prompts”; “all our sources are verified”; “we are already profitable”; “we beat well-funded competitors”; “the tests prove a customer purchased.” Competitor public release cadence makes some of these provably unsound.

**Discovery questions:** Which recommendation affected a recent deal or executive decision? What exactly was uncertain about the source or comparison? Who authorized what correction? Which existing tool or manual process failed? Would a documented second comparable check change a real budget or workflow decision?

## Venture-scale architecture (conditional, not a promised valuation)
- **Stage 0:** founder-operated, evidence-safe SaaS with repeatable paid narrow pilots and legal provider coverage.
- **Stage 1:** repeatable same-role retention; workflow/templates and partner-assisted onboarding; permissioned customer-owned evidence exports.
- **Stage 2:** privacy-governed integrations with customer truth, CRM/analytics and approved enterprise procurement routes if demand materializes; track cohort margins and expansion from verified receipts.
- **Stage 3, only with validated network economics:** a permissioned decision-history layer whose value compounds through customer-controlled review history, integrations and trusted distribution, without selling customer confidential source material.

Illustrative arithmetic, **not an ARR forecast**: 2,000 customers at $500 per month would imply $12 million annual *gross subscription revenue* **only if all paid for 12 months**, before churn, infrastructure, provider licenses, taxes or salaries. A founder holding 25% would require a **$4 billion equity valuation** for an on-paper $1 billion stake; no financing, market multiple, dilution or liquidity is assumed. The operative next milestone is one real paid partner and repeatable retained usage, not optimizing hypothetical billionaire math.

## Research operations and Agent Reach handoff
- The dated nine-row registry `COMPETITIVE-PUBLIC-SIGNALS-2026-09-30.json` contains compact public publisher descriptions, official source URLs, evidence classes and *separate* Foremention hypotheses.
- **Agent Reach was NOT run in this hosted runtime:** its binary was unavailable and local sandbox outbound DNS was unavailable. We used official web and the connected public TinyFish extraction capability for the publicly checkable facts. Never relabel that as Agent Reach collection or first-party customer research.
- The offline, network-free `scripts/competitive-signal-intake.mjs` validates manually supplied NDJSON exports; exact official HTTPS hosts, provenance dates, bounded input, PII/secret filters and duplicate rejection; it emits **human-review-pending** candidates to stdout only, with no GitHub write, CRM ingestion or scraping.
- Local Agent Reach usage may start with its author's **read-only** installation check `agent-reach install --env=auto` and `agent-reach doctor`, after auditing the third-party package. Do not use `--system`, authenticated Instagram/Reddit/Twitter cookies, rate-limit workarounds or paid proxy by default. The official install instructions are https://github.com/Panniantong/agent-reach/blob/main/docs/install.md . Restrict collection to public, permissioned sources; social opinion is a directional field signal, not verified customer demand.
- Sample reviewed *input* line:
```json
{"competitor":"Peec AI","url":"https://peec.ai/blog/introducing-ai-referrals","title":"Peec describes public referral reporting","summary":"The publisher describes an analytics view of AI-assistant referral traffic.","published_on":"2026-09-11","observed_on":"2026-09-30","source_kind":"OFFICIAL_COMPANY_ANNOUNCEMENT"}
```
