## 2. Category definition

**Recommendation Intelligence** is the discipline of observing, preserving, reviewing, and comparing the recommendations AI-mediated buyers receive—tied to the exact buyer question, provider/model context, returned evidence, review state, and measurement conditions—so teams can decide what to do next without confusing presence with proof.

### Why the category exists

SEO measures and improves discoverability in search. GEO/AEO work commonly focuses on making content useful or eligible for generative/answer environments. Rank tracking follows ordered positions. Generic AI visibility can summarize mentions or share of voice.

Recommendation Intelligence answers a different operating question: **What recommendation did the buyer-facing AI system actually return for this defined buying question, what evidence accompanied it, what can we safely conclude, and what changed under an equivalent later measurement?**

### Category manifesto

- Observe before optimizing.
- Preserve the exact buyer question and measurement context.
- Separate returned references, retrievability, observed evidence, human review, and safe conclusions.
- Treat missing, insufficient, contradictory, and not-comparable states as product truth.
- Require review before stronger conclusions.
- Compare only equivalent later measurements.
- Keep observed change separate from causation and business impact.
- Prefer an inspectable Recommendation Record to an opaque composite score.

### Category vocabulary

Canonical vocabulary includes Buyer Question, Recommendation Observation, Recommendation Record, Returned Reference, Distinct Source, Retrievability, Evidence State, Human Review, Safe Conclusion, Recommendation Presence, Source Concentration, Comparable Later Measurement, Not Comparable, Attention, Decision, Action, and Observed Outcome.

Public definitions live at `/glossary`; the category hub lives at `/recommendation-intelligence`.

## 3. Founder-led distribution operating system

### ICP target account schema

Use a CRM record, not a fabricated prospect list. Each target-account record should support:

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `account_id` | internal stable ID |
| `company_name` | real company only after sourced |
| `domain` | verified company domain |
| `segment` | ICP / adjacent / disqualified |
| `employee_band` | evidence-backed band or unknown |
| `arr_band` | only if sourced; otherwise unknown |
| `category` | software category |
| `market` | primary relevant market |
| `champion_role` | likely operator role, not a made-up person |
| `economic_buyer_role` | likely buyer role |
| `trigger_event` | sourced event or none |
| `trigger_source_url` | provenance |
| `trigger_observed_at` | timestamp |
| `research_notes` | factual account context |
| `recommended_angle` | hypothesis, explicitly labeled |
| `outreach_status` | not_started / drafted / reviewed / sent / replied / closed |
| `next_action_at` | follow-up boundary |
| `owner` | internal owner |

### Trigger events

Prioritize verifiable events that create a reason to care now: category repositioning, major product launch, competitive change, search/organic leadership hire, AI-search initiative, new content program, funding, expansion into a new market, executive commentary about AI discovery, or a measurable recommendation gap already observed with permission.

A trigger event must have a source and observation date. “Probably cares about AI” is not a trigger.

### Personalized outreach contract

1. Open with the sourced trigger or relevant company context.
2. State the buyer-question/recommendation problem in the prospect’s category.
3. Offer one inspectable artifact or hypothesis, not a made-up result.
4. Ask for a small next step: review a Record, compare five buyer questions, or discuss a design-partner workflow.
5. Log the message, source, status, and next action.

No fake personalization. No invented recent event. No invented use of Foremention.

### LinkedIn founder content system

Content pillars:

- Category POV: explain Recommendation Intelligence and its boundaries.
- Evidence teardowns: show how to distinguish returned, retrieved, observed, reviewed, and safe-conclusion states using fictional/illustrative examples clearly labeled as such.
- Buyer-question research: publish methods, frameworks, and real aggregate findings only when qualified.
- Model behavior notes: document provider variance and measurement caveats.
- Build-in-public methodology: explain decisions about comparability, evidence, privacy, and human review.
- Customer proof: publish only approved, real customer evidence.

Cadence is secondary to quality. Each post should have one thesis, one evidence boundary, and one useful next question.

### Demo conversion

The demo should move from the prospect’s real category and buyer questions to one Recommendation Record, evidence inspection, the Attention/Questions/Records/Comparisons loop, and a concrete design-partner success criterion. Do not turn the demo into a tour of every contextual route.

### Design partner acquisition

Qualification fields: ICP fit, urgent recommendation-intelligence problem, willingness to supply real buyer questions, ability to run at least two comparable cycles, access to a champion, review participation, security fit, and permission boundaries for any future case study.

The design-partner close should define scope, timebox, questions, providers, competitors, measurement cadence, human-review responsibilities, success criteria, and what data may or may not be used in research.

### Referrals and follow-ups

Ask for a referral only after the customer has received a useful reviewed Record or decision-relevant insight. Follow-ups should reference the prior interaction or new sourced trigger. If nothing changed, do not manufacture urgency.

## 4. Foremention Research

Foremention Research is a methodology-led publication system, not a content mill.

Programs:

- Recommendation Intelligence reports
- model behavior research
- citation and source research
- buyer-question research
- category reports
- annual **State of Recommendation Intelligence** research program
- benchmark reports only after real data qualifies

### Research publication gate

Before publishing a quantitative benchmark, document:

- research question;
- cohort definition;
- minimum sample threshold;
- market/language scope;
- buyer-question identity/version rules;
- provider/model configuration;
- observation window;
- failed-run handling;
- source normalization rules;
- review coverage;
- privacy/aggregation controls;
- comparison eligibility;
- limitations;
- reproducibility notes.

If the cohort is not eligible, publish methodology or a research agenda—not a fake benchmark. Never fabricate a number to make a report feel complete.

## 5. SEO + data distribution architecture

### Canonical topic clusters

1. Recommendation Intelligence — category definition, vocabulary, category boundaries.
2. Recommendation Records — observation, evidence chain, review, sharing, export.
3. Buyer Questions — intent, versioning, research methodology.
4. Recommendation Evidence — citations, distinct sources, retrievability, source review, causal restraint.
5. Comparable Measurement — repeated sampling, provider variance, not-comparable rules.
6. AI-mediated B2B buying — buyer behavior and decision context.
7. Methodology — measurement design, failure handling, privacy, human review.

### Authority surfaces

- `/recommendation-intelligence` — category hub
- `/methodology` — canonical methodology
- `/recommendation-record` — canonical product object explainer
- `/insights` — Foremention Research
- `/glossary` — DefinedTermSet vocabulary
- `/partners` — partner-program architecture

### Structured data

Use conservative Schema.org structures that match visible page content: `WebPage`, `CollectionPage`, and `DefinedTermSet`, plus the existing Organization metadata. Do not add Review, AggregateRating, Dataset, or claim-oriented schema without real matching evidence.

### Internal linking

Public navigation links Product, Category, Methodology, and Research. Footer links Recommendation Record, Glossary, Partners, trust pages, and access routes. Research pages link back to methodology and the category definition.

### Indexation controls

The sitemap is finite and curated. Authenticated `/app/`, tokenized `/share/`, APIs, auth flows, login, and signup are excluded from crawl directives. Shared Records remain `noindex` even when a valid private link exists.

### Programmatic/data SEO gate

Do not generate thousands of thin category/company/question pages. A templated data page is eligible only when it has a stable entity identity, real qualified data, useful unique analysis, provenance, enough coverage to answer a distinct search need, and an explicit index/noindex decision. Otherwise keep it inside the product or research workflow.

### Content QA checklist

Every public claim should pass: source/provenance check, evidence-state check, date/freshness check, causal-language check, privacy check, canonical check, internal-link check, metadata check, structured-data-to-visible-content check, and thin/duplicate-content check.

## 6. Recommendation Record viral loop

Target loop: **Recommendation Record → share → stakeholder/executive view → workspace CTA/invite → new workspace**.

### Existing recovered controls

The current product already uses cryptographically random share tokens, stores only SHA-256 token hashes, supports expiration, supports revocation, limits creation to eligible workspace roles, keeps shared pages read-only and `noindex`, and provides CSV plus Print/PDF export from the authenticated Record flow.

### This build’s extension

- Explicit `stakeholder` view for underlying answer/evidence inspection.
- Explicit `executive` view that withholds detailed answer text/source locations and emphasizes the evidence boundary.
- Branded Foremention attribution on the shared Record.
- Privacy-safe share-view and workspace-CTA analytics without the raw token, customer name, question text, or answer text.
- Workspace creation CTA attributed to `shared-record` without implying that the recipient was invited by a named customer.
- Explicit `visibility` contract: current production sharing supports `private`; requests for `public` fail closed until a reviewed publishing model exists.

### Public-state architecture

A future public Recommendation Record state must be opt-in, separately authorized, content-reviewed, reversible, and safe for search indexing. It should never be inferred from possession of a private share link. Until those controls and customer-facing consent exist, public publishing remains disabled.

### Share analytics

Allowed events: `record_share_created`, `record_share_viewed`, `record_share_workspace_cta_clicked`. Safe properties are limited to booleans/enums such as `include_evidence` and `view_mode`. Never send a share token, raw buyer question, answer text, customer name, email, or source URL to product analytics.

## 7. Partner distribution system

Partner tracks: agencies, consultants, SEO/GEO providers, B2B marketing firms, VC portfolios, accelerators, and integration partners.

Partner CRM schema should record organization, partner type, real contact only when sourced, source/provenance, customer overlap only with permission, proposed use case, data-access boundary, commercial model hypothesis, status, owner, next action, and public-reference permission.

A partner type is not a partnership. Do not fabricate logos, quotes, certifications, integrations, referrals, or pipeline. Public references require explicit approval.

## 8. Conversion architecture

Primary public conversion remains `Request a demo`. Category and research pages educate first, then link to Methodology, Recommendation Record, and Contact. Shared Records use a lower-friction `Create a Foremention workspace` CTA because the recipient already has product context.

Measure page views and CTA clicks, then connect to real signup/workspace activation only through existing internal IDs and PII-safe analytics contracts.

## 9. QA gates

### Conversion
- navigation remains usable on desktop/mobile;
- CTAs resolve to existing routes;
- no homepage visual redesign;
- shared Record CTA does not expose the token in analytics.

### SEO
- one canonical per indexed page;
- finite sitemap;
- private/auth routes excluded;
- structured data matches visible content;
- no unsupported leadership or benchmark claims.

### Security and sharing
- raw share token never persisted or logged;
- private sharing is the only enabled visibility state;
- expiration and revocation remain enforced;
- executive view exposes less detail than stakeholder view;
- workspace data cannot be addressed by a caller-supplied organization ID.

### Analytics
- all new events are allowlisted;
- only safe enum/boolean properties survive sanitization;
- no raw question, answer, customer identity, email, source URL, or share token.

### Production behavior
- exact branch SHA must pass tests, lint, typecheck, build, migration replay, security checks, and browser acceptance before merge;
- production verification must be tied to the exact deployed SHA rather than assumed from source state.

## 10. Success metrics

Category: qualified direct traffic to category/methodology, glossary engagement, branded category mentions measured externally only when sourced.  
Founder distribution: sourced target accounts, reply rate, qualified demos, design partners, follow-up completion.  
Research: qualified publications, citations/backlinks, research-assisted demos, benchmark eligibility—not volume of thin posts.  
Viral loop: shares created, valid shared views, workspace CTA clicks, resulting signups/activations where attribution is permitted.  
Partners: qualified partner conversations, active customer workflows through partners, referrals, and retained revenue with real provenance.
