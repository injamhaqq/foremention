# Foremention — Distribution + Category Operating System

Status: canonical operating guidance for the integrated billion-dollar build. This document describes process and evidence requirements; it does not assert traction.

## Category and product boundary

Foremention is **Recommendation Intelligence** for B2B software.

The canonical signed-in information architecture remains:

**Attention → Questions → Records → Comparisons → Settings**

The **Recommendation Record** is the canonical inspectable object. Evidence inspection stays inside the Record. The evidence lifecycle remains:

**Returned → Retrieved → Observed → Reviewed → Safe Conclusion**

A returned reference is not proof that the source caused a recommendation. Human review and exact comparability remain required before stronger conclusions.

## ICP target account and trigger event

The current ICP target account is a hypothesis, not a proven customer segment: English-language growth-stage B2B software companies where AI-mediated buyer recommendations may affect discovery or shortlisting and a marketing team can act on evidence.

Candidate trigger event examples include a category or positioning launch, competitor movement, an executive request for recommendation evidence, a new-market launch, or a need to prioritize recommendation visibility work. These remain hypotheses until first-party pipeline, interview, activation, and retention evidence validates them.

## Founder-led distribution motion

Founder-led distribution is an operating process, not proof of demand.

1. Identify an ICP target account from lawful first-party or public business context.
2. Record the trigger event or reason for outreach.
3. Use a concise, evidence-bounded first touch through an appropriate channel such as email or LinkedIn.
4. Qualify the problem before promising a product outcome.
5. If appropriate, move the organization into the design partner workflow.
6. Preserve objections, lost reasons, validated use cases, referrals, and verified commercial events in the first-party commercial/customer-proof system.
7. Never turn outreach volume, replies, meetings, applications, checkout attempts, or proposals into revenue or customer claims.

No synthetic prospect, fake reply, fake meeting, fake pipeline movement, or invented customer proof may enter company reporting.

## CRM schema

The CRM schema is an operator architecture, not evidence of customers or revenue. It may represent:

- account identity and qualification context;
- protected contacts and roles;
- opportunity stage and immutable stage history;
- pricing research evidence;
- interviews, objections, feature requests, validated use cases, referrals, lost reasons;
- verified payment, renewal, expansion, or churn events when those events actually occur.

Commercial/contact PII remains outside browser-customer analytics and normal product exposure. Pipeline is not revenue. A configured price is not willingness-to-pay evidence. Checkout is not verified payment.

## Design partner motion

A design partner is a real external organization only after first-party evidence supports that classification. A design-partner application alone does not establish a partnership, customer relationship, payment, retention, or product-market fit.

The narrow intended workflow is:

**configure → five approved buyer questions → real measurement → reviewed Recommendation Record → evidence-backed action → owner → exact-comparable later measurement → decision**

The founder-led motion should learn from activation, second-cycle behavior, interviews, objections, lost reasons, verified payment, renewals, expansions, referrals, and churn. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## Research authority

Foremention may publish methodology and describe research programs before a dataset qualifies. Findings, market statistics, comparative claims, and customer outcomes require an explicit evidence gate.

Research must preserve question identity, provider/model context, methodology, market/language, time window, failure handling, review coverage, and comparison eligibility. Unsupported causal claims remain prohibited.

## Partner program

Partner categories may be described as intended operating tracks for agencies, consultants, SEO/GEO providers, B2B marketing firms, VC portfolios, accelerators, and integration partners. A partner program page does not imply any existing partnership, endorsement, logo permission, customer relationship, or working integration.

Never fabricate partner logos, partner counts, integration status, referral volume, or co-marketing proof.

## Benchmark gate

A benchmark remains withheld until a privacy-safe eligible cohort and documented methodology support it. At minimum, the benchmark gate must define:

- cohort eligibility and exclusions;
- minimum sample threshold;
- question identity and comparison rules;
- provider/model context;
- market and language;
- measurement time window;
- failure and missing-data handling;
- review coverage;
- privacy treatment.

If those conditions are absent, the correct state is **insufficient evidence**, not a synthetic benchmark.

## Sharing and acquisition boundaries

Recommendation Record sharing is private, read-only, expiring, revocable, and tenant-scoped. Public Record publishing is disabled unless a future separately verified capability enables it.

Public acquisition surfaces may explain Recommendation Intelligence, methodology, research, glossary terms, partner operating models, and privacy-safe shared Record views. They must not expose workspace data, private notes, credentials, raw share tokens, or unrelated customer data.

## Analytics contract

Distribution analytics remain allowlisted and PII-safe. They may record bounded events such as category/research/partner page views and privacy-safe shared-record view modes. They must not emit raw questions, raw answers, customer names, commercial contact text, or share tokens.

Legacy telemetry aliases may normalize retired event names internally for continuity, but standalone Source X-Ray is not a product surface, acquisition route, or canonical analytics event.

## Truth rule

**Never fabricate** prospects, customers, partnerships, benchmarks, revenue, pricing validation, ROI, retention, integrations, certifications, investor interest, or market leadership. When evidence is missing, report unknown, unconfigured, insufficient evidence, or not comparable.
