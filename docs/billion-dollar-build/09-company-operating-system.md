# Foremention — Canonical Company Operating System

Status: canonical operating guidance for the integrated billion-dollar build as of August 31, 2026. This document separates repository facts, operating policies, hypotheses, targets, and unknown business outcomes. It does not manufacture company performance.

## Strategy system

### Mission

Help B2B software teams observe, preserve, review, and compare the recommendations AI-mediated buyers receive so they can make evidence-backed decisions without confusing presence with proof.

### Category

**Recommendation Intelligence**

### Product constitution

- Signed-in IA: **Attention → Questions → Records → Comparisons → Settings**.
- Canonical object: **Recommendation Record**.
- Evidence lifecycle: **Returned → Retrieved → Observed → Reviewed → Safe Conclusion**.
- Standalone Source X-Ray is retired; useful evidence inspection remains contained in the Recommendation Record.
- Human review and exact comparability are real decision boundaries.
- Tenant isolation, RLS, provider boundaries, cost controls, privacy-safe analytics, and truthful failure/unknown states are non-negotiable.

### Current wedge hypothesis

The repository supports a narrow recurring workflow:

**configure → five approved buyer questions → real measurement → reviewed Recommendation Record → evidence-backed action → owner → exact-comparable later measurement → decision**

The hypothesis is that this workflow can reduce uncertainty for growth-stage B2B software marketing teams. ICP, economic buyer, willingness to pay, retention, and category demand remain hypotheses until first-party evidence validates them.

### Strategic moat maturity

Potential moat components include accumulated longitudinal Recommendation Records, exact measurement context, reviewed evidence, workflow history, trusted customer outcomes, quality evaluation data, and operational reliability. None should be described as a defensible moat merely because the schema or product capability exists. Moat claims require observed proprietary data depth, retention, workflow dependence, or other evidence.

### Decision principles

1. Evidence before assertion.
2. Preserve exact measurement context before comparing change.
3. Human review before stronger conclusions.
4. Missing evidence is a state, not a reason to estimate.
5. Verified commercial events outrank pipeline or configuration.
6. One canonical truth store beats parallel dashboards or ledgers.
7. Security, privacy, tenant isolation, and provider boundaries cannot be traded away for speed.
8. Build the narrow retained workflow before expanding product surface area.
9. Do not convert hypotheses into public facts through repetition.
10. Prefer reversible experiments until customer evidence supports scale.

### Explicit non-goals

Foremention will not:

- fabricate customers, benchmarks, revenue, ROI, integrations, certifications, partnerships, investor interest, or market leadership;
- manufacture a universal AI rank from generated answers;
- claim a returned source caused a recommendation;
- resurrect Source X-Ray as a standalone product object;
- add hierarchy, automation, internationalization, enterprise controls, or integrations solely to look complete;
- treat checkout, proposals, applications, meetings, or pipeline as verified payment;
- publish cross-customer benchmarks without a privacy-safe eligible cohort and methodology gate.

## North-Star system

Use a small hierarchy based on retained verified decision value rather than vanity traffic.

### Primary operating outcome

**Retained comparable decision cycles** — KPI-eligible accounts that complete a reviewed Recommendation Record workflow and return for a second exact-comparable reviewed cycle.

### Supporting metrics

- activated KPI-eligible accounts;
- first Recommendation Record reviewed;
- evidence-backed actions created;
- actions assigned to an owner;
- second comparable cycles completed;
- retained accounts/cycles where the cohort is mature enough to measure;
- verified paying conversion only when billing/payment evidence exists;
- verified renewal and expansion only when first-party commercial events exist;
- time to first reviewed value and time to second comparable cycle only when minimum real sample gates are met;
- customer-value realization recorded from first-party review/outcome evidence without unsupported causality.

Every metric must preserve its denominator, eligibility rules, observation window, evidence source, and insufficient-data behavior.

## Operating cadence

### Weekly company review

Review only persisted or directly evidenced information:

- activation and retained-cycle signals;
- customer interviews, objections, lost reasons, use cases, referrals;
- qualified pipeline movement without treating it as revenue;
- product/reliability incidents and provider failures;
- spend, reservation, budget, and abnormal-usage controls;
- security or trust changes;
- highest-confidence decision for the next week.

### Weekly product review

Inspect Attention, Questions, Records, Comparisons, and Settings for workflow friction. Review evidence quality, AI evaluation results, exact-comparability failures, migration/runtime errors, browser acceptance, accessibility, and cost/reliability regressions before adding surface area.

### Monthly finance review

Classify every financial value as one of:

- **ACTUAL** — verified historical value;
- **CONTRACTED** — supported by an executed commercial obligation;
- **FORECAST** — model output based on explicit inputs;
- **ASSUMPTION** — unverified modeling input;
- **TARGET** — desired future state;
- **UNKNOWN** — not supported by available evidence.

The 36-month model may include customers, new logos, ACV, expansion, churn, ARR/MRR, recognized revenue, provider/infrastructure cost, payroll, sales/marketing, G&A, gross margin, burn, cash, and runway. Formulas do not turn unknown inputs into actuals.

### Quarterly strategy review

Revisit ICP, wedge, category demand, retained workflow, packaging, pricing evidence, win/loss patterns, reliability, gross-margin evidence, security requirements, hiring triggers, and international demand. Preserve decisions and the evidence that changed them.

## Talent and hiring triggers

Hire from bottlenecks and risk, not optimistic dates.

Examples of evidence-based triggers:

- engineering capacity becomes the persistent constraint on validated customer workflow;
- AI/data quality requires dedicated evaluation or data operations beyond the current owner’s sustainable capacity;
- reliability/security obligations exceed safe founder/generalist ownership;
- repeated onboarding/support load demonstrates a customer-success role is economically justified;
- qualified pipeline and repeatable sales motion justify dedicated sales capacity;
- finance/operations complexity creates material reporting, compliance, or control risk.

Headcount plans are targets or assumptions until people are actually engaged. Do not invent employees or compensation actuals.

## International readiness

International expansion is gated by demonstrated demand and operational readiness, not translation volume.

Evaluate each market on:

- customer demand and revenue evidence;
- provider/model availability and measurement quality;
- language quality and evaluation coverage;
- locale, date, time, timezone, number, and currency behavior;
- legal/privacy/compliance complexity;
- support burden;
- payment/billing readiness;
- data-residency requirements;
- competitive environment;
- localization cost.

Do not claim international PMF, residency, localized quality, or regulatory readiness until verified.

## Documentation precedence

When documents disagree, use this order:

1. live production/code/configuration evidence for implementation facts;
2. current repository constitution and security/release rules;
3. current canonical billion-dollar-build operating docs;
4. accepted integration history and tests;
5. older branch plans, handoffs, and specialist PR documents.

Historical documents remain useful provenance but cannot silently override newer integrated truth.

## Company truth rule

No operating system may turn a plan into traction. Customer count, paid pilots, ARR/MRR, ACV, churn, expansion, CAC, LTV, gross margin, burn, runway, certifications, residency, partnerships, benchmarks, or market validation remain **UNKNOWN** unless first-party evidence supports a stronger classification.
