# Ten-interview product validation sprint

Foremention is testing a self-serve recommendation-intelligence wedge: lean B2B SaaS teams that care about inbound discovery and have seen competitors appear in AI buying answers.

Synthetic scores, compliments, and signups are not buying evidence. This sprint is designed to test whether customers will repeatedly use and pay for the software.

## Recruit

Interview ten people over two weeks:

- Founder, head of marketing, growth lead, content lead, or product marketing lead
- B2B SaaS, preferably marketing or sales software
- Roughly 5–50 employees
- Established competitors
- Meaningful inbound, review, comparison, or content activity
- Has personally checked AI recommendations or received an internal screenshot

Do not recruit only friends or people who already support the thesis.

## Interview sequence

1. “Tell me about the last time an AI answer affected a marketing or sales discussion.”
2. “What exact question was asked, and what did the answer show?”
3. “What did your team do next?”
4. “How do you currently monitor AI recommendations?”
5. “Which part is hardest: knowing you are absent, knowing why, or deciding what to change?”
6. “What sources or comparisons seem to influence those answers?”
7. “How often would you need this evidence refreshed?”
8. “What evidence would leadership need before paying for a tool?”
9. “Would you rather start with 10 monthly questions, 100 weekly questions, or a larger daily-monitoring plan?”
10. “After using a working demo, would you pay $49/month or $199/month? What would have to be true?”

Avoid pitching until the behavioral questions are complete.

## Evidence to capture

- Exact triggering incident
- Existing tool or workaround
- Time or money already spent
- User, champion, and budget owner
- Required proof and refresh frequency
- Security, compliance, and reputation objections
- Must-have integration or export
- Pricing reaction after product use
- Permission for a follow-up pilot

## Pass / fail gate

Continue when:

- At least six of ten describe the problem without prompting.
- At least four use a recurring workaround or pay for an adjacent product.
- At least five complete the seeded product journey without assistance.
- At least three agree to connect a real brand and prompt set.
- At least two start a paid subscription or provide an equivalent written purchase commitment.

Pause or narrow the segment when fewer than three interviews show active recurring behavior. Do not treat curiosity or a waitlist signup as willingness to pay.

## Product-loop test

For each pilot, measure:

- Time to first useful Source Map
- Percentage of approved questions with a completed observation
- Weekly active workspaces
- Evidence pages opened per session
- Opportunity actions recorded
- Four-week retention
- Upgrade intent at the plan limit

The product must deliver value without founder analysis between collection and the customer’s decision.

## Sprint output

After ten interviews, record repeated problem language, buying triggers, objections, activation and retention data, paid commitments, segment changes, and one decision: continue, narrow, or stop.
