Why evidence chains matter

AI recommendation monitoring becomes trustworthy only when every observation can be inspected later. Exact provider and model labels make later comparisons trustworthy. Teams should know which system answered, when it answered, and which methodology produced the observation.

Returned citation URLs remain attached to the answer record. That makes a later source review auditable without pretending that a crawler discovered the same source independently.

Human review stays separate from automated page observations. A page can be reachable and still require a person to decide whether the brand appears, whether competitors appear, and whether a legitimate action exists.
