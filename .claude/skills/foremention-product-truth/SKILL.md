## Product truth chain

`buyer question -> provider/model -> answer -> named/recommended brand -> returned reference when available -> distinct source -> retrievability -> evidence -> human review -> diagnosis -> Change Specification -> human decision -> execution artifact(s) when needed -> comparable later measurement -> observed business outcome -> learning`

Keep observation, inference, automation, review, acceptance, rejection, unavailable evidence, insufficient evidence, contradiction, comparability, later observations, outcomes, and causation-not-proven distinct.

A returned source is not proof that the source caused the recommendation. A controllable company action does not control an AI provider's ranking, model weights, reasoning, personalization, future updates, competitor actions, or future prompts.

Change Specification drafts may remain incomplete. Never invent owner, effort, priority, exact change, dependencies, scope, controllable surface, or confidence merely to complete a record. Safe generated-draft defaults are Eligibility `UNKNOWN`, Decision `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, Truth `HYPOTHESIS`, and Confidence `INSUFFICIENT` until inspectable evidence and human review justify stronger states.

Historical Resolution Assets remain valid execution records and must not be backfilled with fabricated Change Specifications. New Recommendation Engineering execution assets are subordinate to reviewed Change Specifications.

## Truth and commercial restraint

Never fabricate customers, logos, ARR/MRR, user counts, investor names, funding, testimonials, reviews, benchmarks, accuracy claims, recommendation counts, or case studies. Use Demo Data / Illustrative Example / Placeholder labels where needed.

Do not overclaim causality. `Prove` means inspect what can actually be substantiated. Category Leadership OS is a destination, not proof of category leadership, demand, PMF, revenue, retention, willingness to pay, ROI, or causal impact.
