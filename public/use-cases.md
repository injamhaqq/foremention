# Recommendation Intelligence use cases

Foremention is recommendation intelligence for B2B software. These use cases describe current product workflows and decision boundaries; they are not ranking guarantees, customer outcomes, or claims that Foremention controls an AI provider's future answers.

## 1. Observe an important buyer question

Version a buyer question that can affect a shortlist, record the approved measurement surface, preserve the observed answer, named brands, returned references, model context, and timestamp, and keep the result as a Recommendation Record.

**Decision boundary:** one answer is an observation, not proof of a stable market pattern.

## 2. Understand a competitor recommendation without guessing why

Inspect the observed answer and its returned evidence, then compare those observations with verified Company Truth and eligibility.

**Decision boundary:** evidence, inference, and structural product differences stay separate. A competitor gap can remain unknown.

## 3. Review sources attached to a recommendation

Keep the returned reference distinct from the source destination, test retrievability, inspect observed evidence, record human review state, and preserve limitations.

**Decision boundary:** a returned or reviewed source does not by itself prove that the source caused the recommendation.

## 4. Turn a reviewed gap into an exact company change

Create a Change Specification stating the proposed customer-owned change, supporting evidence, owner, acceptance criteria, and verification plan.

**Decision boundary:** a human approves, tests, rejects, monitors, or leaves the change unresolved before execution artifacts are created.

## 5. Remeasure later under comparable conditions

Repeat the observation only when the buyer-question version, provider/model context, collection method, and other material measurement conditions remain equivalent enough for comparison.

**Decision boundary:** observed movement is not proof that a company action caused that movement.

## Current live measurement surface

The current free-only collection path uses **Cloudflare Workers AI with independently retrieved Bing Search RSS sources and grounded synthesis**. It is not direct monitoring of ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity consumer applications. Broader provider surfaces require separate provenance, rights, cost-control, and comparability validation before coverage is claimed.
