# Recommendation Intelligence

Recommendation Intelligence is the discipline of observing, preserving, reviewing and comparing the recommendations AI-mediated buyers receive. The observation is tied to a versioned buyer question, provider/model, timestamp, returned evidence, human review and comparable measurement context so teams can make an informed next decision without confusing visibility with proof.

This describes Foremention's current product thesis, not proof of market leadership, universal adoption or verified customer results.

## Why the discipline exists

Teams can already measure web search, website analytics and pipeline. When a potential buyer asks an AI system what products to consider, a distinct measurement question arises: which products appeared in that answer, what references were returned, what evidence is genuinely inspectable, and did an equivalent later observation change?

The analysis starts with the observed answer and its evidence limitations, not assumptions about hidden model reasoning.

## How it differs from related work

- **SEO:** improves how web content can be discovered or understood through search; Recommendation Intelligence measures a specific AI-mediated buyer-facing answer.
- **GEO and AEO:** usually emphasize suitability for generative answers; Recommendation Intelligence records actual provider outputs and does not claim that a particular optimization caused them.
- **Rank tracking:** follows positions in a defined search result set. Generated answers do not provide an automatically comparable universal rank, so Foremention preserves response context instead.
- **AI visibility:** may summarize brand mentions or share of voice. Recommendation Intelligence attaches every claim to its exact buyer question, Recommendation Record, source provenance, review boundaries and valid later comparison.

## Shared vocabulary

- **Buyer question:** the versioned input and decision context for the observation.
- **Recommendation observation:** the provider's actual answer and named or recommended vendors at a timestamp.
- **Recommendation Record:** the inspectable object joining the question identity, provider/model, answer, returned references, evidence state, human review and comparison conditions.
- **Evidence state:** Returned → Retrieved → Observed → Reviewed → Safe conclusion.
- **Comparable later measurement:** a subsequent observation aligned to the relevant question, model, methodology, market and other material conditions.
- **Attention:** a decision-relevant change, uncertainty, review requirement or evidence-linked follow-up.

## Core operating principles

Observe before optimizing. Preserve context. Separate returned citations from retrieved sources and human-reviewed evidence. Keep missing and non-comparable states visible. Require review before stronger company conclusions. Do not upgrade observed recommendation movement to a claim about business impact or causation without independent proof.
