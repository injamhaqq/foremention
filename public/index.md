# Know what your company should change next to become the stronger recommendation.

Foremention is recommendation intelligence for B2B software. It observes how AI systems answer buyers' questions, preserves the evidence behind those recommendations, separates controllable gaps from structural ones, and turns reviewed gaps into exact company changes a team can evaluate and verify.

No fake reviews. No hidden promotion. No ranking guarantees. No fabricated scores. No causal claims without evidence.

[Apply as Design Partner](https://foremention.com/contact)

## Recommendation Record

A Recommendation Record is a timestamped measurement object. It keeps the buyer question, provider and model context, returned answer, returned references, source retrievability, review state, uncertainty, and later-comparison eligibility together.

A returned or reviewed source does not, by itself, prove that the source caused the recommendation.

[Inspect a Recommendation Record](https://foremention.com/recommendation-record)

## From buyer question to company change

1. Start with a real buyer question.
2. Preserve the recommendation observation.
3. Keep the evidence and uncertainty attached.
4. Compare the observation with Company Truth.
5. Determine eligibility.
6. Define a Change Specification.
7. Require human approval.
8. Record what was actually executed.
9. Remeasure only under comparable conditions.
10. Record learning without manufacturing causality.

## Company Truth and eligibility

Foremention separates verified product, integration, pricing, security, market, policy, use-case, and proof facts from hypotheses. It can identify a requirement as eligible, partially eligible, structurally ineligible, or unknown. Sometimes the correct decision is to make no company change.

## Design partner cycle

The current design-partner workflow uses one real B2B software category, up to five important buyer questions, a baseline, evidence review, one exact customer-owned Change Specification, implementation only if approved by the customer, and comparable later remeasurement.

Applying does not create a paid subscription, guarantee an outcome, or authorize a company change.
